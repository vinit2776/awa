The seven stages

Everything AWA does is one of these, in this order. The bold name is what we call it on screen; the grey name is what auditors and paperwork call it.

  1. 01

    Someone asks for something

    requisition yours

    Describe what you need and which budget it belongs to. Nothing is ordered and no money moves.

  2. 02

    Someone with authority agrees

    approval yours

    Agree, ask a question, or send it back for changes. Sending back is not a refusal — the same request comes round again.

  3. 05

    Somebody confirms it turned up

    goods receipt whoever takes delivery

    Not you, if you asked for it — deliberately, so nobody can order and sign for their own purchase.
