05 · Approvals inbox  —  src/app/dashboard/approvals/page.tsx

Dashboard›Approvals

Approvals

Requisitions waiting on a decision from you. Nothing here is visible to other approvers until you have decided — a multi-step chain opens one step at a time.

Your four options, and what each one does

  1. Approve hands the requisition on — to the next approver if there is one, or to sourcing if you are the last.
  2. Asking a question holds it exactly where it is. The requester keeps their place in the queue and nothing is rejected.
  3. Sending it back lets the requester edit and resubmit. Approval then restarts from the first step, including you.

07 · Sourcing / Fulfillment / Payment queue  —  the three pipeline pages, same shape

Sourcing

Turn approved requisitions into purchase orders — either by inviting vendors to quote, or by ordering directly when the vendor and price are already settled.

How sourcing works

  1. Raise an RFQ to invite two or more vendors to quote, or skip straight to a purchase order.
  2. Compare the quotations side by side.
  3. Select one to issue the purchase order.

Nothing to source yet

Requisitions arrive here the moment their last approver signs off. 2 are in approval right now.

Fulfillment

Record what actually arrived. Purchase orders land here once issued and stay until every line is received or accepted — then the vendor can invoice against them.
